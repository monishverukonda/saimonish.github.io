Profile

A planner who builds with geospatial data

I trained as a civil engineer and an urban & regional planner, then specialised in remote sensing and GIS at the Indian Institute of Remote Sensing (ISRO). My work sits where those fields meet.

I have prepared GIS-based master plans and urban infrastructure studies for municipalities, led a 20-member team delivering web and mobile GIS for government clients, and now process LiDAR, UAV and mobile-mapping data into terrain models, 3D products and GIS databases at Fugro in Saudi Arabia.

I care about accurate data, clear maps and documentation that decision makers can act on.
